Health Benefits of Tennis

  • Tennis improves heart health and strengthens the cardiovascular system.
  • Playing tennis helps build muscle strength in the legs, arms, and core.
  • Tennis can burn between 400–600 calories per hour.
  • The sport improves balance, agility, and hand-eye coordination.
  • Playing tennis regularly can reduce stress and improve mental health.
  • Tennis helps improve focus, reaction time, and decision-making.

Tennis Facts

  • Tennis originated in France in the late 19th century.
  • A professional tennis match can last over 5 hours.
  • The four biggest tournaments are called the Grand Slams.
  • Tennis can be played as singles or doubles.
